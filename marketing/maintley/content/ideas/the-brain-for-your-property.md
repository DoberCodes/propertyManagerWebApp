## Post

What if your property could remember?

Not in a complicated way.

Just the practical things that matter:

- What work was done.
- When it happened.
- Who did it.
- Which documents belong with it.
- What should be easier to find next time.

That is the idea behind Maintley.

It helps your property keep a useful memory, so maintenance history, documents, warranties, and records do not disappear into scattered folders or forgotten conversations.
