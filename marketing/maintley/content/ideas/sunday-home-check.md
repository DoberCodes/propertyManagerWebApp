## Post

Sunday Home Check:

Pick one small thing around the house and make the record easier for future you.

- Take a photo of a warranty or receipt.
- Write down a filter size.
- Add the date of the last service visit.
- Save the name of a contractor you would call again.

None of this has to be perfect.

The goal is to make the next repair, replacement, or maintenance decision a little easier.
