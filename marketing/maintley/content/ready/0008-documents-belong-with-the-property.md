# Problem

Home documents are often stored wherever they happened to arrive, making them difficult to connect to the system or repair they explain.

---

# Story / Evidence

Manuals, warranties, invoices, inspection reports, and receipts may be split across email, folders, photos, and paper files.

---

# Maintley Connection

Maintley helps homeowners keep documents with the property records they support.

---

# Recommended Post

The right document is most useful when it is connected to the right part of the home.

A warranty helps more when it is tied to the appliance.

A receipt helps more when it is tied to the repair.

A manual helps more when it is easy to find before a service visit.

The problem is that home documents usually end up wherever they first arrived: email, paper folders, text messages, photo rolls, or a drawer in the kitchen.

Maintley helps homeowners keep documents, warranties, receipts, photos, and maintenance history connected to the property, so important records are easier to find when they matter.
