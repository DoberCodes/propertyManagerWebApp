# Summary

This post helps homeowners see that small records saved today, such as dates, receipts, photos, warranties, and service notes, become more valuable as the home ages and memory fades.

---

# Problem

Homeowners often think a maintenance record only matters when something breaks. In reality, the details that seem small today are usually the same details that become hard to find later.

---

# Story / Evidence

A homeowner may remember that the HVAC was serviced "sometime last year," but not the exact date, company, invoice, warranty terms, or filter size. That may not feel important until the next service call, a warranty question, a home sale, or a repeat repair.

The issue is not neglect. It is that homes collect information slowly, across years, and most people do not have one place where that information can settle.
