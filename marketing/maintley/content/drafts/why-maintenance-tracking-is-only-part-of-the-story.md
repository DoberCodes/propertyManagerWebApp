## Post

Maintenance tracking is useful.

But it is only part of the story.

A completed task answers one question:

What got done?

A property record answers more:

- When was it done?
- Who worked on it?
- What documents or receipts are connected?
- Is there warranty information?
- What should be easier to remember next time?

That is why Maintley is built around long-term property records, not just task lists.

The work matters. The history matters too.
