## Post

Most homeowners do not know what is missing from their property records until they need it.

The water heater model number.

The date the HVAC was last serviced.

The receipt for a repair that is still under warranty.

The contractor who fixed the issue last time.

Maintley helps turn those unknowns into simple next steps. It reviews what you have saved and points out records that could make future maintenance easier.

The point is not to grade your home.

The point is to help you build a more useful record over time.
