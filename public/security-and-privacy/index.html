Security and control

How Maintley protects your home maintenance records

Maintley is responsible for protecting the software and enforcing access. You remain in control of the property information you choose to keep in it.

Start free Read the Privacy Policy

Your information remains yours

You retain ownership of the information you enter. Maintley receives only the permission needed to store and process that information to operate the service.

Maintley is designed for useful property records such as maintenance history, equipment details, documents, warranties, photos, and reminders. It is not intended to become an unnecessary personal profile.

Login and permission checks work together

Firebase Authentication identifies the person signing in. Authentication alone does not grant access to property records.

Account membership

Maintley checks whether a person belongs to the account that owns or manages the records.

Roles and assignments

Available actions can be limited by account role and by assignment to particular properties.

Server-enforced rules

Firestore, Storage, and server checks enforce access boundaries instead of relying only on whether a button is visible.

Outside access is not automatic

Maintley does not automatically give contractors or service businesses access to a property. A person must have the appropriate account and property permissions before they can view or change records.

Maintenance records are attributed to the person or organization that submitted them when that information is available. Attribution explains who recorded something; it does not mean Maintley verified the work or certified that it was completed correctly.

Storage and operational safeguards

Maintley uses Firebase services for authentication, property records, and uploaded files. Access rules are tied to account and property permissions, and administrative support systems are separated from ordinary customer roles.

No online service can promise absolute security. Maintley maintains practical controls, limits access according to implemented permissions, and asks users to protect their credentials and report suspected account compromise promptly.

Exports, corrections, and deletion

  • Users can correct the information they have entered when the related workflow supports editing.
  • Maintley supports property and maintenance reports in formats such as PDF or CSV.
  • Exports are informational and are not certified for legal, insurance, resale, or regulatory use.
  • Users may request account and associated-data deletion, subject to legal and operational limitations described in the Privacy Policy.

Questions, privacy requests, or a suspected issue

Email maintleyapp@gmail.com for privacy questions, deletion requests, or suspected account compromise. Do not include passwords, payment card details, or unnecessary sensitive information in email.

Policies and product explanations serve different purposes

This page explains Maintley's current product controls in plain language. The Legal Hub, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Service provide the governing legal terms.
