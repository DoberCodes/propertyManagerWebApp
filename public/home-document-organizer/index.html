Frequently asked questions

What home documents should homeowners keep?

Homeowners should keep warranties, manuals, invoices, receipts, inspection reports, permits, photos, service records, and important property documents.

Why connect documents to equipment and maintenance history?

Documents are easier to use when they are attached to the related home, equipment profile, maintenance task, or service record.

Can Maintley organize warranties and manuals?

Yes. Maintley helps organize warranties, manuals, invoices, receipts, photos, and other files around the property and equipment records they belong to.
