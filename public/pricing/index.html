Start free, then add depth when your records need it

Maintley is designed so the first useful record can be simple: a task, an equipment profile, a document, or a completed maintenance note. Paid plan options are intended for homes and portfolios that need richer history, reports, documents, team access, and long-term visibility.

  • Track maintenance tasks, recurring reminders, and completed work.
  • Keep equipment records, warranties, manuals, invoices, and photos connected to the home.
  • Review reports and costs when maintenance history needs to be shared or exported.
  • Use team and property workflows when a home or portfolio needs more coordination.
