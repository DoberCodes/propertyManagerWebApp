Home maintenance checklist by season

Updated July 2026 by Maintley

A seasonal home maintenance checklist helps routine care happen before it becomes urgent. The best checklist is not just a list of chores. It should become part of your home maintenance history so future you can see what was done, when it was completed, and what needs attention next.

Spring

  • Inspect gutters, downspouts, drainage, and exterior grading.
  • Check roof areas, siding, windows, and exterior caulking for visible issues.
  • Service HVAC before heavy cooling season when possible.
  • Test outdoor faucets, irrigation, sump pumps, and drainage paths.
  • Log any photos, repairs, or contractor recommendations.

Summer

  • Replace or inspect HVAC filters based on your actual usage.
  • Clean dryer lint areas and check exterior vents.
  • Inspect decks, railings, exterior stairs, and outdoor lighting.
  • Review appliance and equipment records for missing warranty or model details.

Fall

  • Prepare heating systems and schedule service before cold weather.
  • Clean gutters after leaf drop and inspect roof drainage.
  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors.
  • Check weatherstripping, exterior gaps, and freeze-prone plumbing areas.

Winter

  • Monitor heating performance and unusual equipment behavior.
  • Check for drafts, moisture, leaks, and frozen-pipe risks.
  • Review your maintenance history and plan recurring tasks for the year ahead.
