Frequently asked questions

What appliance details should homeowners track?

Homeowners should track the make, type, model number, serial number, install date, warranty information, service history, documents, photos, and recurring care details for major appliances and home equipment.

Can Maintley track HVAC filters and recurring appliance care?

Yes. Maintley can track equipment profiles, filter details, recurring maintenance reminders, completed service history, and documents connected to the appliance or system.

Why keep appliance records with maintenance history?

Keeping appliance records with maintenance history makes future repairs easier because the service record, documents, parts, warranty details, and past work stay connected.
