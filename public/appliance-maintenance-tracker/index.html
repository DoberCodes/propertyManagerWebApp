Know what you own and what has been done

Appliance maintenance is hard to manage when details live in different places. A model number may be on a photo, the warranty may be in an email, the last repair may be on an invoice, and the filter size may be written on a box in the garage.

Maintley gives each appliance and equipment record a home. Save install dates, service history, parts, documents, photos, and notes so the next repair or replacement starts with context.
