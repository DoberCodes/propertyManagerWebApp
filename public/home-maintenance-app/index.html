Built for more than a checklist

A good home maintenance app should help you decide what to do next, but it should also preserve what already happened. Maintley keeps upcoming work and completed maintenance connected so your home record becomes more useful over time.

  • See overdue, upcoming, and recurring maintenance tasks.
  • Save completed work as service history.
  • Track equipment details, filter sizes, model numbers, and serial numbers.
  • Keep warranties, manuals, invoices, receipts, photos, and notes organized.
  • Use saved history to make future reminders and recommendations more helpful.

What Maintley helps you manage

Home reminders

Create recurring care for filters, safety checks, water heaters, exterior work, seasonal upkeep, and routine tasks.

Home records

Equipment memory

Frequently asked questions

What should a home maintenance app track?

A home maintenance app should track upcoming tasks, recurring reminders, completed work, equipment details, warranties, manuals, invoices, photos, costs, and notes.

Can Maintley track both reminders and history?

Yes. Maintley tracks upcoming maintenance reminders and turns completed work into home maintenance history.

Is Maintley available on Android?

Yes. Maintley is available for Android on Google Play and also supports web access.
