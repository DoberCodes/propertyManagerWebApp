Frequently asked questions

What should be included in a home maintenance log?

A home maintenance log should include the date, description, cost, contractor or person who completed the work, related equipment, documents, photos, receipts, invoices, and any follow-up work.

Can a maintenance log help with future repairs?

Yes. A maintenance log helps future repairs by showing what was done before, who did the work, which equipment was involved, and what recommendations or documents were saved.

Does Maintley connect completed tasks to maintenance history?

Yes. Maintley is designed so completed tasks and service records become part of the home's maintenance history.
