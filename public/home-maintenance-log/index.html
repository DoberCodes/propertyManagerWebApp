A log that keeps context, not just dates

A good home maintenance log should tell you more than that something happened. It should preserve the date, description, cost, contractor, documents, photos, linked equipment, and follow-up work that make the record useful later.

Maintley connects completed work to the home and the equipment it belongs to, helping you answer questions months or years later.

  • Record completed maintenance tasks and repairs.
  • Add service notes, invoices, receipts, and photos.
  • Connect maintenance history to HVAC, appliances, water heaters, and other equipment.
  • Review recent maintenance or a full chronological timeline.
  • Use history to improve reminders and future planning.

Useful for homeowners and property owners

Homeowners

Know what has been done, what documents exist, and when routine maintenance last happened.

Landlords

Keep service records, contractor work, invoices, and property maintenance history organized across rentals.

Future owners

Maintain a record that can explain how the home was cared for over time.
