A maintenance tracker built around the home itself

Most home maintenance tools only focus on the next task. Maintley focuses on the record you are building over time. A completed filter change, a water heater flush, a contractor invoice, a roof inspection photo, and a warranty document all become part of the same home history.

That history matters later. When an appliance fails, when a contractor asks what was done last time, or when you need to remember a filter size, Maintley keeps the answer attached to the right home and equipment record.

  • Create one-time and recurring home maintenance tasks.
  • Log completed repairs and routine service.
  • Keep warranties, manuals, invoices, photos, and notes organized.
  • Track equipment details like install dates, model numbers, serial numbers, and filter sizes.
  • Review maintenance history when planning future care.

Frequently asked questions

What is a home maintenance tracker?

A home maintenance tracker helps you record upcoming tasks, completed work, equipment details, documents, and recurring reminders for your home.

Can Maintley track both tasks and history?

Yes. Maintley tracks open tasks and completed maintenance records so the work you finish becomes part of the long-term home history.

Is Maintley only for landlords?

No. Maintley is homeowner-first, but it also supports property owners and landlords who need better maintenance records across multiple homes.
