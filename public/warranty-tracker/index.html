Warranty tracking needs more than a file upload

Common warranty records to keep

Purchase records

Receipts, invoices, purchase dates, costs, contractor details, and installation notes.

Equipment details

Make, type, model number, serial number, install date, filter size, part details, and photos.

Service history

Completed maintenance, repair notes, contractor visits, invoices, and documents that may support a claim.

Frequently asked questions

What warranty information should homeowners track?

Homeowners should track warranty documents, purchase receipts, install dates, expiration dates, model numbers, serial numbers, manuals, service history, and related equipment details.

Why connect warranties to equipment records?

A warranty is easier to use when it is connected to the equipment, install date, receipt, manual, and service history that may be needed for a claim.

Can Maintley store warranty documents and receipts?

Yes. Maintley helps store warranty documents, manuals, receipts, invoices, photos, and service records with the related property and equipment record.
