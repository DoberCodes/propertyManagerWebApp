Frequently asked questions

What maintenance reminders should homeowners create?

Common reminders include HVAC filter checks, dryer lint care, smoke and carbon monoxide detector tests, water heater service, gutter checks, seasonal inspections, and appliance maintenance.

Can recurring reminders connect to home maintenance history?

Yes. Maintley keeps recurring reminders connected to tasks, completed work, equipment records, and maintenance history so reminders stay useful over time.

Why adjust recurring maintenance schedules?

Recurring schedules should match how the home is actually maintained. Adjusting schedules can keep reminders aligned with usage, service history, and seasonal needs.
