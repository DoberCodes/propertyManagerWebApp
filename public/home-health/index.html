Home Health

See what your home needs, what changed recently, and what records are missing

Home Health is Maintley's way of turning maintenance records into a clearer view of your home's condition, recent activity, open work, and long-term record quality.

Start building Home Health

A healthier home record starts with better maintenance memory

Home Health is not a home inspection score. It is a practical way to understand whether your property records are helping you make better decisions. If maintenance history is missing, reminders are stale, equipment profiles are incomplete, or documents are scattered, the home becomes harder to manage.

Maintley brings those signals together so you can see what needs attention and what is already well documented.
